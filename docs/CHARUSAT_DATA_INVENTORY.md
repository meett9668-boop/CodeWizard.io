## 5. DATA NEEDED FOR FULL ROOM-LEVEL NAVIGATION

To expand the navigation graph from prototype demonstration nodes to comprehensive campus room-level routing, the following physical data items are required by the graph router:

### 1. Verified Room Coordinates
* **Requirement**: Exact relative $(x, y)$ map coordinates for room doorways.
* **Why Required**: A* needs accurate node coordinates to calculate the spatial Haversine heuristic $h(n)$ and render polyline coordinates cleanly on indoor floor overlays.

### 2. Floor-Plan Geometry
* **Requirement**: Vector SVG/GeoJSON bounding polygons of building floor plans.
* **Why Required**: Essential for rendering indoor map layers in Leaflet and ensuring paths don't route visually through walls.

### 3. Corridor Junctions
* **Requirement**: Node placement at every corridor intersection, turn, and hallway branch.
* **Why Required**: Corridors serve as decision points in graph traversal; without junction nodes, edge distance calculations $g(n)$ become inaccurate.

### 4. Door/Entrance Locations
* **Requirement**: Exact coordinates of exterior building doors and interior room access points.
* **Why Required**: Defines the transition boundary edge (`edge_type: "entrance"`) between outdoor paths and indoor corridors.

### 5. Staircases & Core Locations
* **Requirement**: Physical location of all stairwells per floor.
* **Why Required**: Represent vertical non-accessible edges (`has_stairs: true`). The router requires exact stairwell nodes to connect adjacent floor levels.

### 6. Elevators
* **Requirement**: Physical location of all operational elevators per floor.
* **Why Required**: Represent vertical accessible edges (`accessible: true`). Required by the A* engine when operating in `ACCESSIBLE` mode to bypass stairs.

### 7. Ramps
* **Requirement**: Location and slope gradient of exterior and interior ramps.
* **Why Required**: Ground-level step-free entrance points that allow wheelchair/mobility routing to access building lobbies.

### 8. Accessible Connections
* **Requirement**: Operational verification of step-free paths, automatic doors, and elevator width compliance.
* **Why Required**: Enforces strict safety rules so the router never routes an accessible user down an inaccessible branch.

### 9. Room-to-Corridor Connections
* **Requirement**: Direct edges connecting room door nodes to their nearest corridor junction node.
* **Why Required**: Completes the last 10 meters of graph traversal, allowing pathfinding to terminate directly at the room doorway.

### 10. Inter-Building Outdoor Paths
* **Requirement**: Measured pathway edges connecting outdoor building entrances (e.g. A5 to A6, A6 to A7).
* **Why Required**: Allows multi-building campus routing when a student navigates from a lab in DEPSTAR (A5) to a classroom in CSPIT Block 2 (A7).
