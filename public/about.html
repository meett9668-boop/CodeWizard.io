Problem Statement #19 · Campus Navigator

We built a campus OS, not just a map.

CAMPUSLY is a hackathon project solving room-level campus navigation for CHARUSAT University using graph-based A* pathfinding.

"Google Maps tells you how to reach the campus.
CAMPUSLY tells you how to reach Room 638."

The Problem

Large university campuses act as micro-cities. Standard mapping applications treat buildings as opaque boxes, directing students only to general coordinates. This leads to confusion, particularly for freshmen, guests, and differently-abled individuals who need to locate specific rooms, labs, or accessible facilities.

The Solution

CAMPUSLY breaks buildings open. By digitizing floor plans into weighted mathematical graphs and applying optimized A* routing algorithms, we provide door-to-door indoor navigation. Users get precise floor-by-floor instructions, and our adaptive cost functions ensure step-free accessibility.

⚄

Graph Engine

Campus locations become nodes. Walking paths become weighted edges. This fundamental data structure allows us to calculate absolute shortest paths dynamically.

⚡

A* Algorithm

f(n) = g(n) + h(n). Our implementation evaluates both exact path cost and spatial Haversine heuristic estimates to the destination.

♿

Accessibility Matrix

Stair edges are strictly filtered out when accessibility mode is active. Elevators and ramps are automatically preferred.

🏢

Indoor Mapping

We leverage indoor mapping principles: isolating building levels, rooms, corridors, stairs, and elevators to provide context-aware instructions.
